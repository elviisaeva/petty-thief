# Lens: learn

What is the idea, and how do I go deeper?

## Look at
- **Core idea** in three lines.
- **Mental model:** a picture, analogy or rule of thumb that makes it stick.
- **What's new:** compared with what is common knowledge on the topic.
- **Sources:** the primary source plus 1–3 good places to go deeper, all checked to exist.

## Verdict extras
- **One exercise:** a 15-minute task that turns this into a skill.
