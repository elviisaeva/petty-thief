# Lens: creator

How is this made, and why does it work?

## Look at
- **Hook:** the first 1–2 seconds or the first slide, quoted verbatim (up to 15 words), and why it grabs attention.
- **Structure:** by second or by slide (hook → promise → body → turn → CTA or loop).
- **On-screen text:** style, size, placement, words per screen.
- **Editing** (only if frames were seen): length, number of cuts, average shot length, zooms, transitions, music or voice, b-roll, screen recording.
- **Visuals:** cover, light, color, fonts, face on camera or not.
- **CTA and comments:** what people argue about or ask, and the words that repeat.
- **Numbers:** views, likes, comments, saves, compared with the author's usual numbers if visible. Otherwise "not visible".

## Verdict extras
- **Steal this:** the one technique worth reusing, described so it can be reused without copying.
- **Your hook:** one hook in the profile's language for the user's own take, written the way a person talks.
