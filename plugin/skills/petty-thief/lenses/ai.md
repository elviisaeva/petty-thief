# Lens: ai

What is the workflow, and what is real?

## Look at
- **The setup:** tools, models, prompts, agents and the order of steps.
- **Claims vs reality:** every claim ("10x faster", "fully automatic", "just launched") checked at the primary source. Unchecked claims go under "Not verified".
- **Cost, keys and privacy:** paid plans, API keys, and where the data goes.
- **Try it in 10 minutes:** the smallest version the user could run today.
- **Failure modes:** where this breaks, and what the video skipped.

## Verdict extras
- **Worth trying?** Yes / Later / No, with one sentence why.
